Bottle stands upright slightly right of center on pale beige travertine block, tiny soft sage leaf branch entering lower left, muted cream-sage seamless studio background with warm sunlight and crisp botanical cast shadows. Leave top quarter calm empty background for HTML heading overlay. No additional text, no graphic decorations, no border, no collage.
